Simple process

From JD to ranked candidates in 4 steps

No manual question writing. No spreadsheet juggling. Just upload, generate, invite, and rank.

Talvyn 20-Second Platform Walkthrough

01

Upload your job description

Paste any JD or upload a PDF/Word file. Our AI extracts the required skills, seniority level, and role context automatically.

02

Generate role-specific tests

Talvyn instantly creates three custom assessments — Technical, Reasoning, and Problem-Solving — tailored to the exact skills in your JD.

03

Invite candidates

Send a unique test link to each candidate by email. They complete the assessment on any device, no account needed.

04

Review ranked results

See every candidate ranked by AI-evaluated score with an instant hire recommendation, detailed breakdown, and exportable report.
